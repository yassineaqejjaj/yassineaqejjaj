AI-native engineering

My delivery method

01 · Define

Business outcome, acceptance criteria, users, constraints and risks.

02 · Architect

Bounded services, interfaces, memory, security and cost trade-offs.

03 · Build & review

AI-assisted engineering, manual design choices, CI and test suites.

04 · Measure

Traces, baselines, regressions, evidence and known limitations.

A public repository or demo does not establish production readiness. Clinical, education and compliance claims require dedicated validation. Case studies identify planned measurements separately from completed tests.
